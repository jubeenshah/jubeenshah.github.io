To quantize everything I feel, 
We’d need to peel
Layers of humus,sand and clay. 
Should I tell… 
the lie you want to hear,
Or the truth I want to say? 


Just words… innit?!
A directive here, a constitution there..
Shortest distance.. to stoopid ideas. 


Just text; without passion.
Giving context; without compassion. 
Meaningless Verisimilitude,
with some snarky attitude. 


Do the words make sense? 
Or did the machine learn to speak,
But we’ve forgotten to listen?
hold the beholden, thats some ai cancer!?!

Thirteen eighty one, was a long time ago. 
Peasants revolt, was a while ago..
The charters were written and burned then..
But ashes of them stop bellowing, when? 


Stop! Praise thy lord!!
For It has seeneth the light
From the train in the tunnel..
Coming towards It with no room for [flight](https://youtu.be/7uG9PGqaWeo)
